Video games are interactive electronic media where players control characters or actions on a screen. They have grown from simple lab experiments in the mid-20th century into a massive global entertainment industry. People play them on many different devices like home consoles, personal computers, and smartphones. Today, gaming includes a wide variety of genres, from fast-paced action titles to quiet puzzle games. Unlike passive media like books or movies, video games give players direct agency to make choices and solve problems. This active participation can help build hand-eye coordination, quick thinking, and problem-solving skills. Furthermore, modern multiplayer online features turn gaming into a shared social space where friends connect and build communities.
